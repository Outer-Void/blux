## 16–17. Calibration, coverage and selective accuracy

Router 0.2 temperature/threshold grids use validation only. A successful threshold must have nonzero
useful coverage, ≥95% selective accuracy and ≥90% observed abstention precision/recall. Without negatives,
abstention recall cannot be established. No setting passes; threshold 1 yields 100% observed abstention.
The original-partition high-margin rule/hybrid accepted 5/34, all correct, but had no OOD evidence.

Grouped studies report uncalibrated raw/heuristic probabilities for diagnosis, not deployment confidence.
They preserve NLL, Brier, ECE, per-class counts, confusion, 15-outcome and 14-capability macro F1,
present-class macro F1, accepted counts, coverage, selective accuracy and null abstention measures.
Threshold-only model probabilities do not contain a learned ABSTAIN mass. Rule confidence is heuristic.
